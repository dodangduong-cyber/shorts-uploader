Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Shorts Uploader is a desktop program that runs only on its owner's own Windows PC. It has no servers, no website back end and no user accounts. This policy explains what data the program handles and what it does with it.

Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google, the program asks for these permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload). Used to upload finished Shorts to your channel and receive each new video's ID.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). Used only to read your channel's handle, so the program can refuse to upload if you are signed in to the wrong channel.

The program does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, email address or any other Google data.

How we store Google data

  • Your OAuth access token and refresh token are saved in a file named token.json in the program's folder on your PC. They are used only to call the YouTube Data API.
  • A local log file, uploads.jsonl, records each upload's time, video ID, title and hype score.
  • This data stays on your PC. It is sent only to Google's APIs, over encrypted HTTPS connections, to perform the uploads you authorized.

How we share Google data

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Shorts Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Other data the program handles

  • Twitch chat: the program reads the public chat of the stream anonymously and keeps only a count of messages per second, in memory. It does not store message text or usernames.
  • Stream video: the live stream is recorded to a rolling buffer on your PC, and recordings are deleted after about 10 minutes. Finished clips are kept in the program's clips folder until you delete them.
  • Captions: speech is transcribed on your PC. Audio is not sent to any outside service.

Retention and deletion

  • Google tokens are kept until you delete token.json or revoke access.
  • You can revoke the program's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the saved tokens stop working.
  • To remove all local data, delete token.json, uploads.jsonl and the clips and buffer folders.
  • Uploaded videos can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
