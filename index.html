Shorts Uploader

Live stream highlights to YouTube Shorts, automatically.

What it does

Shorts Uploader is a desktop program that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. While a Twitch stream is live, it:

  1. records the stream to a short rolling buffer on the PC,
  2. measures how fast the public chat is moving to spot hype moments,
  3. cuts about 30 seconds around each moment and makes a vertical video with captions and music,
  4. uploads the best clips as YouTube Shorts to the owner's channel, @JunkoCrazyMoments.

How it uses your Google account

The program asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read the signed-in channel's handle. It reads the handle only to confirm that it is uploading to the right channel. It does nothing else with your Google account.

Who it is for

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own channel. It is not a public service and has no sign-ups.
